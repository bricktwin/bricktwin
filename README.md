# BrickTwin

BrickTwin is a snap-together figure made of 3D printed parts, kind of like building your own brick character.

## Who I Am

I was 11 when I started this and I'm the co-founder of BrickTwin.

## Why You'd Want One

It's a fun process to put together something that looks like you, and it makes a good gift.

## How It Gets Made

We're going to use AI to generate their facial and physical features, sort of like a Funko Pop style. Then the parts get made on a 3D printer and ship as a kit you put together yourself. The figure is about 4 inches tall, made of 8 parts, printed in your skin tone, and everything snaps together with no glue.

## My Job

I work on the 3D printers and help generate ideas. I'll also help with some of the coding and other things of that matter. My dad handles the legal stuff and the technical backend.

## The Plan

In a year I hope we'll have made at least a few sales.
